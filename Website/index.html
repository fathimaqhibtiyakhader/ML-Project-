Overview

The Challenge

Heart disease needs early identification. Real clinical data has missing values, duplicates and outliers, and different feature scales reduce model reliability.

Our Objective

Analyse the dataset through EDA, apply preprocessing (duplicate removal, imputation, scaling) and build Ridge, LASSO and Elastic Net classifiers.

Case-Based Scenario

A 55-year-old patient visits with chest discomfort and shortness of breath. Age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, maximum heart rate, exercise-induced angina and more are recorded, preprocessed and passed to the ML model, which predicts whether heart disease is likely.

Data Preprocessing

1. Remove duplicates

5 repeated rows dropped.

2. Median imputation

Robust fill for missing values.

3. StandardScaler

Equal footing for the regularization penalty.

4. 80:20 stratified split

Class proportions preserved.

Imputation and scaling run inside a pipeline, so they are fitted on training data only. This prevents data leakage.

Algorithms

Ridge (L2)

Penalises large coefficients and controls complexity, especially with correlated features.

LASSO (L1)

Can shrink coefficients to zero, which helps identify less important features.

Elastic Net

Combines L1 and L2 penalties (l1_ratio = 0.5).
